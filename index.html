About The Hollow Choir

Our Story

In the depths of 2024, when the world grew quiet and silence became deafening, four souls discovered an abandoned cathedral deep within the digital realm. Its vaulted ceilings held echoes of forgotten hymns, and its stained-glass windows pulsed with residual energy from countless forgotten dreams.

The Vocalist, drawn by the cathedral's haunting acoustics, found her voice could awaken the sleeping harmonies dormant in these ancient walls. The Guitarist discovered that his strings could summon the shadows dancing in the cathedral's corners, while the Keyboardist learned to channel the cathedral's ghostly choir through his keys. The Drummer became the heartbeat of this sacred space, his rhythms echoing like the footsteps of spirits long departed.

Together, they became The Hollow Choir - not just four musicians, but conduits for the voices trapped between worlds. Their music is a bridge between the living and the forgotten, a lighthouse in the void where lost souls find their chorus. Each note they play is a story whispered from beyond the veil, each harmony a prayer answered by the echoes themselves.
